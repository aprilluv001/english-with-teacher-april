Hi, I'm Teacher April!

I have over six years of English teaching experience, both online and face-to-face. I've taught more than 1000 students, from young children to adults, with different English levels and cultural backgrounds.

I have experience teaching learners from countries including Japan, China, and Korea. I also adapt my teaching approach to suit individual learning needs.

My lessons focus on speaking, grammar, vocabulary, reading, pronunciation, and practical communication. I use guided conversations, visual materials, activities, and real-life examples to make learning more comfortable and engaging.

As a licensed professional teacher, I aim to create a patient and supportive learning environment where students can practice English without being afraid of making mistakes.

"You don't have to speak perfect English to begin. You just need the confidence to keep learning."

Mizuki’s Journey

Mizuki Morita, a learner from Japan, has been my student for over two years. Throughout this time, I’ve had the privilege of witnessing his growth—not only in English, but also in his academic and professional journey.

I first met him while he was pursuing his PhD, and I’m incredibly proud to have witnessed him reach such an amazing milestone: becoming a licensed doctor with a Doctor of Science.

Over the years, our relationship has grown beyond that of a student and teacher. Through our lessons and countless conversations, we’ve built a genuine friendship, and I’m truly grateful for the connection we’ve developed along the way.

I’ve watched his confidence, communication skills, and English ability grow tremendously. His dedication, perseverance, and passion for learning have always inspired me. Being able to witness his journey from pursuing his PhD to becoming a doctor has been such a meaningful experience for me.

I’m grateful that our paths crossed, and even more grateful that what started as a student-teacher relationship has grown into a friendship that I truly value. 💙
